Medical Book Editor & Proofreader Needed (2 Books, Under 40,000 Words Total)

I am looking for an experienced editor and proofreader (one person) to edit and proofread two nonfiction medical books with a combined total word count of under 40,000 words.

Both books have already been professionally medically reviewed, so I am not looking for medical fact-checking. Your role is to ensure the writing is polished, clear, engaging, and publication-ready for a general audience.

Responsibilities

* Copy edit for grammar, spelling, punctuation, and syntax.
* Improve sentence flow, readability, and clarity while preserving the author's voice.
* Ensure consistency in wording, capitalization, formatting, and style throughout both books.
* Proofread the final manuscripts to eliminate any remaining errors.
* Use **Track Changes** in Microsoft Word so all edits are visible.

## Requirements

* Excellent command of **U.S. English** with native-level proficiency.
* Proven experience editing nonfiction books (medical or health-related experience is preferred).
* Strong attention to detail and consistency.
* Ability to deliver polished, publication-ready manuscripts.

Project Details

* Two nonfiction medical books.
* Combined word count: under 40,000 words.
* Content has already been professionally medically reviewed.

Budget

Fixed price: $200 USD, paid upon successful completion of the project.

To Apply

Please include:

* A brief summary of your editing experience.
* Examples of similar projects, if available.
* Your estimated turnaround time.
