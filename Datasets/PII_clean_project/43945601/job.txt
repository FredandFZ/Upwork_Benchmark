Typography-Focused Designer for VELLVÉ

job_description:
Hi there! 

I'm looking for a typography-focused designer to help refine—not reinvent—the wordmark for my luxury longevity brand, Vellvé.

Over the past several months, I've developed the creative direction through extensive exploration and mood boards. I'm not looking for new concepts or a completely different logo. What I need now is someone with a strong eye for typography, spacing, proportion, and detail to transform the current direction into a polished, proprietary wordmark.

The brand is inspired by the quiet luxury of Aman, Brunello Cucinelli, Fountain Life, Ingold, and high-end hospitality rather than traditional healthcare or med spas.

The current direction includes:

* A custom V system with a larger first V and smaller second V
* Warm champagne metallic accents
* Sculptural, architectural lettering
* Roman inscription influence without fashion-serif characteristics
* Uniform stroke weights
* A timeless, bespoke feel that communicates optimization, longevity, and elevated wellness

The scope would include:
• Refining the typography and letterforms
• Perfecting kerning and spacing
• Creating a production-ready vector wordmark
• Delivering AI, SVG, EPS, transparent PNGs, and black/white/metallic versions
• A version with and without the "Wellness elevated." tagline

I'll provide mood boards, reference images, and the current logo direction to build from.

Before moving forward, I'd love to see examples of luxury wordmarks or typography-focused branding you've created.

My budget is approximately $300-500.

If this sounds like a good fit, I'd love to connect.
