job_name:
Mechanical Design Engineer Needed

job_description:
I'm looking for an experienced Mechanical Design Engineer with strong SolidWorks skills to help develop a mechanical product from concept through manufacturing-ready CAD.

This is a real product development project—not simply creating a 3D model. I'm looking for someone who understands engineering principles, manufacturing methods, tolerances, and Design for Manufacturing (DFM).

The ideal candidate has experience designing products that have progressed from concept to prototype and production.

Scope of Work:
• Develop complete 3D CAD model in SolidWorks
• Create parametric parts and assemblies
• Design functional mechanical components
• Optimize for DFMA
• Create production-ready engineering drawings
• Apply GD&T where required
• Select standard hardware and fasteners
• Perform tolerance analysis
• Advise on manufacturing processes
• Support prototype revisions

Deliverables:
• SolidWorks Part (.SLDPRT)
• SolidWorks Assembly (.SLDASM)
• STEP files
• Engineering drawings (PDF & DWG)
• Exploded assembly views
• Bill of Materials (BOM)
• Manufacturing-ready documentation
• Source files
Preferred Experience
• SolidWorks
