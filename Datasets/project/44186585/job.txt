- Verify improvement with before/after performance data
- Brief written summary of cause and fix

Requirements
- Proven experience fixing WordPress site speed issues (not just plugin installation)
- Comfortable with WP Engine hosting environment
- Able to read and act on tools like WebPageTest, GTmetrix, Lighthouse
