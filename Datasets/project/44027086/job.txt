Visual Strategy Consultant for Nonfiction Book

job_description:
I’m in the final stages of a nonfiction book on the indoor active entertainment industry (trampoline and adventure parks) and am looking for a visual strategy consultant to support a short engagement.

The manuscript is complete. I’m not looking for graphic design or full layout, but for strategic guidance on how visuals should support the narrative.

This includes reviewing the manuscript and image tracker, identifying where visuals add value or should be removed, recommending placement, and suggesting where additional visuals could improve clarity or authority.

Ideal candidates have experience with editorial or book-related visual work and understand how visuals enhance long-form content.
